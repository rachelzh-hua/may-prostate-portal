Spatial regression of the prostate after castration · day 10 vs day 20

Abstract
An interactive portal for exploring the spatial transcriptomics of the castrated mouse prostate. It covers 8 10x Visium sections (15,439 spots, whole transcriptome) of C57BL/6 prostate at day 10 and day 20 after orchiectomy, 4 biological replicates per timepoint. Spots are annotated by marker-based compartment scoring, and scored for the androgen-response, luminal/basal identity, gland-involution, stromal and immune programs, so you can see where the androgen axis collapses as the gland regresses.

Data & Reproducibility
Analysis-ready AnnData (.h5ad) per section — X = log1p(CP10k) over the curated portal gene set, layers['counts'] = raw counts, with compartment labels, signature/pathway scores, niche labels and spatial/X_umap embeddings. Load with sc.read_h5ad(). Full raw matrices (19,465 genes) and H&E images are at GEO GSE295043.
Integrated Analysis Tools
Spatial Domain Map
Visium spots on the aligned H&E section (toggle with ◍ H&E), plus a per-section transcriptome UMAP.
Marker-Based Compartments
Leiden domains labelled by relative enrichment: luminal / basal epithelium, stroma, smooth muscle, endothelium, immune, urothelium, seminal vesicle.
Androgen-Axis Scoring
Androgen-response program (Ar, Pbsn, Nkx3-1, Fkbp5, Tmprss2, Msmb, Sbp) scored per 55 µm spot.
Involution & Plasticity
Gland-involution / apoptosis (Clu, Bax, Cdkn1a), basal-identity and neuroendocrine-plasticity (Chga, Syp, Ascl1) scoring.
Gene Expression Search
Color maps by any curated gene; distribution histogram + per-compartment violins.
A / B Differential Compare
Lasso two regions or populations; compare composition, signatures and top differential genes.
Spatial Niches
k-means over each spot's neighborhood composition, pooled across all 8 sections (Color → Spatial niche) — microenvironments, not single labels.
Neighborhood Enrichment
squidpy spatial-graph permutation test (⬡ Neighbors): which compartments co-localize — e.g. immune spots beside regressing glands.
How to Use
1. Browse sections — click a section in the left rail; badges show the timepoint (D10 / D20) and replicate.
2. Change coloring — use the Color dropdown: compartment, spatial niche, a signature, or a pathway-activity score (AR signaling, involution, TGF-β, NF-κB, hypoxia, neuroendocrine …) to see where a program is active.
3. Search a gene — type a gene in the header Gene field (try Tgm4, Pbsn, Clu).
4. Select two subsets — pick bucket A or B, click Lasso, and draw on the map. Selections persist across sections.
5. Compare — once A and B are set, click Compare A / B for composition, signature/pathway and differential-gene analysis.
6. Integrated Atlas — the Atlas tab holds the Harmony-integrated 15,439-spot UMAP: luminal / stromal / immune compartments, composition by timepoint, and compartment markers.
7. Pathways — the Paths tab shows the compartment × pathway activity heatmap and the day-20-vs-day-10 Δ-activity contrast.
8. Timecourse — the D10/D20 tab holds the section-level contrast: programme and composition deltas plus precomputed pseudobulk DE (n = 4 vs 4).
Provenance & caveats
Independent re-analysis, not a reproduction. Sections are processed with Scanpy (log1p → HVG → PCA → Leiden → UMAP) and annotated by marker-based scoring; the Atlas tab adds a Harmony integration over all 8 sections with compartment heuristics. This is not the published pipeline of Shelley, May et al. (Neoplasia 2025), and none of the labels here are the paper's. The Paths tab scores 17 curated gene sets per spot (control-corrected mean, scanpy score_genes — not permutation-tested GSEA/ORA); Δ-activity is descriptive.
A Visium spot is 55 µm and multi-cell: a "compartment" is the dominant program of a spot, not a cell-type call. There is no deconvolution in this portal (no matched mouse-prostate scRNA reference was used), and no intact control exists in this deposit — every section is castrated, so day 10 vs day 20 is the only condition axis.
The day-10 / day-20 contrast is confounded by what each section contains. The day-20 sections carry far less glandular prostate and much more urothelium / seminal vesicle / stroma, so pseudobulk DE separates them almost completely (9,346 of 13,781 genes at FDR<0.05, n=4 vs 4) — that is composition plus biology, not a clean timepoint effect. Read the timecourse tab as descriptive.
Castration-relevant genes are searchable in the Gene box: Tgm4, Pbsn, Sbp (secretory, day-10 high), Clu (involution), Upk2/Krt5 (urothelium / basal).
